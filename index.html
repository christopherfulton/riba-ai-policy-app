Practice Guidance: Artificial Intelligence

This site helps you produce an Artificial Intelligence policy for your Architecture practice, in line with RIBA's Core Principles of AI Use guidance, by following a suggested template. It covers ethics, data governance, intellectual property, client communication and more.


Before you start

Read the RIBA Position Statement on AI, and associated Core Principles of AI Use guidance document (link to follow...)

Start building your AI policy →

After you've built your policy

  • Consider how you will roll out this policy in your practice, leading change and fostering a successful culture of technology use.
  • Line up practice-wide or targeted CPD as necessary to ensure staff have the required understanding to implement this policy.
  • Make a plan for a regular policy review cadence (e.g. yearly), as well as plan for receiving Permitted Use requests from staff.
